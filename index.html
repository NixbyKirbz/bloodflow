Key Structures & Exact PDF Definitions

Exact PDF Text
1. Plasma (55% Percentage by volume) Water 91% | Proteins 7% | Other solutes 2%

Plasma (percentage by weight): • Proteins 7%: Albumins 58%, Globulins 38%, Fibrinogen 4% • Water 91% • Other solutes 2%: Ions, Nutrients, Waste products, Gases, Regulatory substances.

2. Red blood cells (4.2–6.2 million per cubic mm) Formed elements (45%)

Red blood cells: 4.2–6.2 million per cubic mm. Lineage: Myeloid stem cell → Proerythroblast → Early erythroblast → Intermediate erythroblast → Late erythroblast → (Nucleus extruded) → Reticulocyte → Red blood cell.

3. Platelets (250–400 thousand per cubic mm) Buffy coat / Formed elements

Platelets: 250–400 thousand per cubic mm. Lineage: Myeloid stem cell → Megakaryoblast → Megakaryocyte → Megakaryocyte breakup → Platelets.

4. Neutrophils (60%–70% of White blood cells) White blood cells: 5–10 thousand

Neutrophils: 60%–70%. Lineage: Myeloid stem cell → Myeloblast → Progranulocyte → Neutrophilic myelocyte → Neutrophilic band cell → Neutrophil.

5. Lymphocytes (20%–25% of White blood cells) Lymphoid Lineage

Lymphocytes: 20%–25%. Lineage: Lymphoid stem cell → Lymphoblast → Lymphocyte.

6. Monocytes (3%–8% of White blood cells) Myeloid Lineage

Monocytes: 3%–8%. Lineage: Myeloid stem cell → Monoblast → Monocyte.

7. Eosinophils (2%–4%) & Basophils (0.5%–1%) Granulocytes

Eosinophils: 2%–4% (Eosinophilic myelocyte → Eosinophilic band cell → Eosinophil). Basophils: 0.5%–1% (Basophilic myelocyte → Basophilic band cell → Basophil).
