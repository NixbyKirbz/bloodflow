Heart & Blood Vessels Study Guide

Functions of the Heart (4)

Poster Top-Left
1. Generates Blood Pressure

Contractions of the heart chambers generate blood pressure that forces blood through the blood vessels.

2. Routes Blood

Separates pulmonary and systemic circulations so oxygen-poor blood goes to the lungs and oxygen-rich blood goes to body tissues.

3. Ensures One-Way Flow

Heart valves (Tricuspid, Pulmonary Semilunar, Bicuspid, Aortic Semilunar) prevent backward flow of blood.

4. Regulates Blood Supply

Changes in contraction rate and force match the changing metabolic needs of tissues.

ARTERIES "AWAY" from Heart
  • • Thick walls with lots of elastic tissue.
  • • Carry blood AWAY from the heart.
  • • Blood is typically oxygenated (rich in oxygen), except Pulmonary Arteries.
VEINS "TOWARD" the Heart
  • • Carry blood TOWARD the heart.
  • • Less elastic and have thinner walls than arteries.
  • • Where blood is drained from the tissues.
CAPILLARIES "EXCHANGE" Site
  • • Exchange occurs BETWEEN blood and tissue fluids.
  • • Extremely thin walls that allow oxygen, nutrients, and waste products to move between blood & surrounding tissues.
